About Me

I'm Aidarous Mohamed, a results-driven full-stack and mobile developer based in Hargeisa, Somaliland, working primarily across React Native and Flutter on the client side and Node.js/Express with PostgreSQL on the backend.

I led a team to 1st place at the University of Hargeisa Hackathon with a full-stack ride-sharing platform built in 48 hours, and most of my freelance work since has followed the same pattern — complete products rather than isolated features: a production real-time chat app for Android, election dashboards, and enterprise logistics and inventory systems, usually as the sole developer covering architecture, backend integrations, and deployment.

I also spent a year volunteering as a full-stack instructor, teaching the same stack I build with day to day.
